Easy loose script

“It’s live in a phone preview so you can scroll it like a customer. Take a look and tell me if you want it live for real.”

Leads + CRM workflow

Every lead already has a phone number, a deployed website, and a pitch page URL. You call the phone, then send the pitch page.

1

Open the sales call list

Use data/leads/final_lists/sales_call_list.csv (787 rows). Columns include business name, phone, niche, location, rating, deployed site URL, and pitch_url.

2

Call the number on file

Ask for the owner, manager, or decision maker. Do not pitch the receptionist for 5 minutes — get transfer first.

3

Send the pitch page immediately

Each lead has a unique pitch page like https://pmb2.github.io/generated-websites/pitch/<business-slug>/. That page embeds their real website in a phone mockup and has the $295/m CTA.

4

Stay on the line while they open it

Walk them through the hero, services, reviews, and contact. The pitch page is designed for the call: “Open in new tab” + CTA banner after 3 seconds.

5

Close to checkout

CTA goes to Stripe Payment Link. After payment, operations launches the site (domain, DNS, hosting). Log outcome in the CRM / call sheet.
