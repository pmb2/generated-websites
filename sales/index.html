Day-one workflow

This is the whole job. Do these five steps over and over.

1

Open your lead list

Use the sales call list you were given. Every row has a phone number and a pitch page URL.

2

Call and ask for the owner

Ask for the owner, manager, or decision maker. Do not pitch the front desk for five minutes.

3

Send the pitch page right away

Text or email the lead’s pitch URL. It already shows their real website and the $295/mo checkout.

4

Stay on the line while they open it

Walk them through the site. Let them scroll the phone preview. Answer price and ownership simply.

5

Close to checkout

Point them to the Go Live button. After they pay, operations launches the site. Log the result.
