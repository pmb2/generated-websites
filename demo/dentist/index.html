About us

A local team that treats your home like our own

Brightside Family Dental opened in 2012 with a simple mission: give Troy the dental practice we would want for our own families. Dr. Priya Raman built the practice around two ideas that are rare in dentistry, take time with every patient and never recommend a treatment you would not choose for your own mother. Fourteen years and thousands of smiles later, the practice has grown to a team of eleven, but every patient is still seen by the doctor, every plan is still explained in plain English, and every single visit still ends with the same question: is there anything else we can do for you today?

We would rather earn one customer for life than a hundred one-time calls.

FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Do you accept my insurance?

We are in-network with most major plans, including Delta Dental, MetLife, Guardian, and Cigna. Our front desk will verify your benefits before your first visit and give you a clear breakdown of what is covered.

Do you offer sedation for anxious patients?

Yes. We offer nitrous oxide (laughing gas) and oral sedation for patients who need extra comfort. Many of our patients tell us they wish they had come years earlier.

What if I have a dental emergency?

Call (518) 555-0193 as soon as possible. We reserve same-day slots for emergencies and our team will triage your situation over the phone so you know exactly what to do before you arrive.

How often should I get a cleaning?

For most patients, every six months. If you have gum disease or other risk factors, we may recommend every three to four months, and we will explain exactly why.

Do you see children?

Absolutely, and we love it. We recommend a first visit by age three, or earlier if you have concerns. Our pediatric-trained team makes it a positive experience from the start.

What if I cannot afford a big treatment?

We offer payment plans through third-party financing with 0% options, and we will always show you all of your options, including the ones that cost less.
