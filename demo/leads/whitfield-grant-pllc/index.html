FAQ

Questions we hear every day

What does a consultation cost?

Your first consultation is free. Personal injury matters are handled on a contingency basis, and many other matters are offered at a flat fee, so you know your cost before you commit.

How do you bill for your services?

Hourly, flat fee, or contingency, explained to you in writing before work begins. You will never see a surprise line item on a bill from this firm.

Will I work with an associate or a partner?

Your file is handled directly by Margaret Callahan or David Reed. We are a small firm by design, which means you never get passed to a junior associate you have never met.

How long will my case take?

Every matter is different. At your first meeting we will give you an honest timeline based on your specific facts, and we will keep you updated at every milestone.

Do you handle cases outside Saratoga Springs?

Yes. We represent clients across the Capital Region and upstate New York, and we are admitted to practice in New York State and federal courts.

What should I bring to a first meeting?

Any documents related to your matter: contracts, correspondence, police reports, court papers, or insurance documents. If you are not sure what is relevant, bring what you have and we will sort it out together.
