FAQ

Questions we hear every day

How fast can you get to an emergency?

We average under one hour across Schenectady, Niskayuna, Rotterdam, and Glenville, and under 90 minutes for the wider Capital Region. Call (518) 555-0128 and a live dispatcher will send the nearest on-call plumber.

Do you charge extra for nights and weekends?

No. One flat rate, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. You get the exact price before we start, and that price does not change at 2 a.m.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. We are licensed, bonded, and fully insured in New York State, and every job is backed by a 2-year workmanship warranty.

Why is my water heater making noise?

Rumbling or popping usually means sediment has built up at the bottom of the tank. Flushed early, it is a cheap fix. Left alone, it shortens the tank’s life. We will inspect and give you straight options.

How long does a water heater last?

A traditional tank lasts 8–12 years with yearly flushing. Tankless units often last 20+. If yours is past that range, replacement is usually the smart money, and we will show you the math.

Do you offer financing?

Yes. We offer 12-month 0% financing on water heaters, sewer work, and remodel plumbing, with approvals in minutes.
