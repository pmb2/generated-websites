What we do

Law Firm services built around you

Every job starts with a conversation, not a sales pitch. Here is how Kouray & Kouray can help.

DWI / DWAI Defense

A DWI charge can cost you your license, your job, and your freedom. With over four decades of DWI defense experience, we attack every element of the prosecution's case — from the traffic stop to the breath test.

  • 45+ years of DWI defense
  • Challenge stop & test procedures
  • License suspension hearings

Felony Defense

Felony charges carry serious prison time. We build aggressive, thorough defenses and fight to keep charges reduced or dismissed — often before trial.

  • Aggressive pre-trial advocacy
  • Suppression & motion practice
  • Trial-tested litigation

Misdemeanor Defense

A misdemeanor on your record follows you for life. We fight to protect your record, your job, and your future.

  • Reduction & dismissal strategies
  • ACD & diversion programs
  • Record protection

Drug Charges

From possession to sales allegations, drug charges demand experienced counsel. We challenge unlawful searches and fight for the best possible outcome.

  • Search & seizure challenges
  • Treatment alternatives
  • Aggressive representation

Traffic & Vehicle Matters

Suspended licenses, serious violations, and commercial driver issues — we protect your driving record and your livelihood.

  • License restoration
  • Commercial driver defense
  • Points & fines reduction

Personal Injury

Injured in an accident? We fight insurance companies to get you the compensation you deserve — medical bills, lost wages, and pain and suffering.

  • No fee unless we win
  • Car, truck & slip-and-fall
  • Full-value negotiations

About us

A local team that treats your home like our own

Steven X. Kouray has been a fixture of the Schenectady legal community since being admitted to the New York bar in 1976. Over four decades, he has built a reputation as one of the most tenacious trial attorneys in the Capital Region — a lawyer clients describe as a pit bull who goes straight for the jugular. His firm, Kouray & Kouray, handles the cases that matter most: criminal charges, DWIs, and serious personal injury claims. Clients are never left in the dark: the firm promptly returns calls, emails, and faxes, and never misses a deadline.
