Medical Spa services built around you

Every job starts with a conversation, not a sales pitch. Here is how Aurelle Aesthetics & Spa can help.

Botox & Neurotoxins

Smooth fine lines and prevent new ones with precise, natural-looking results. Our nurse injectors use conservative dosing so you still look like you, just refreshed.

  • Results in 3–5 days, lasts 3–4 months
  • Frown lines, forehead & crow’s feet
  • Gentle numbing, minimal downtime

Dermal Fillers

Restore volume, define lips, and soften deep folds with hyaluronic acid fillers placed by an experienced hand. Start subtle, build over time.

  • Lips, cheeks & jawline contouring
  • Allergan & Galderma certified injectors
  • Dissolvable, fully reversible

Laser Skin Resurfacing

Erase sun damage, melasma, and texture with fractionated laser treatments that trigger your skin’s own collagen production.

  • Fractional CO2 & Halo technology
  • Tackles tone, texture & pores
  • Visible improvement after one session

Microneedling & PRP

The ultimate skin reset. Microneedling plus your own platelet-rich plasma stimulates collagen for smoother, firmer, more radiant skin.

  • The natural glow treatment
  • Fine lines, scars & enlarged pores
  • No downtime, back to life same day

Chemical Peels & Medical Facials

From gentle lunchtime peels to deeper resurfacing, we customize every treatment to your skin type and your calendar.

  • Customized to your skin type
  • Brightens dull, tired complexions

About us

A local team that treats your home like our own

Aurelle was born in 2017 from a frustration Alyssa Fontaine felt after a decade as a hospital ICU nurse and aesthetic nurse: too many med spas were pushing volume over artistry, and too many clients were walking out looking overdone. She built Aurelle around the opposite philosophy. Treat skin like the medical specialty it is, design every plan around the individual, and never sell a treatment a client does not need. Nine years later, Aurelle has grown into one of the most-reviewed medical spas in the Capital Region, but the treatment rooms still feel like a sanctuary, and the advice is still the same: less is always more, when it is done right.

We would rather earn one customer for life than a hundred one-time calls.

FAQ

Questions we hear every day

Are your treatments safe?

Yes. Every treatment is performed by a registered nurse under the supervision of our board-certified medical director. We use only FDA-approved products from Allergan, Galderma, and Merz, and we review your medical history before every procedure.

How much downtime should I expect?

Most injectables and facials have zero downtime. Laser resurfacing and deeper peels may involve 2–5 days of peeling. We will always give you a realistic timeline before you book, not after.

How long do results last?

Neurotoxins last 3–4 months, fillers 12–18 months depending on product and placement, and collagen-stimulating treatments build over 3–6 months. Your plan is designed around your maintenance goals and budget.

Who will be doing my treatment?

A registered nurse injector with advanced certification (CANS) and over 50,000 treatments performed. You will never be treated by someone you have not met at your consultation.

Do you offer financing?

Yes, through Cherry and CareCredit with 0% interest options. We also offer membership pricing on injectables for our regular guests.

What should I expect at my first visit?

A relaxed, pressure-free consultation. We assess your skin, listen to your goals, and build a plan together. You will leave with a clear roadmap and no obligation to book anything.
